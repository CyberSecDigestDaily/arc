    Change log

      How it works. Prices and stock are re-checked about four times a day from UK shops (via PriceSpy UK and the shops themselves). Every morning a sweep re-verifies the build, fixes dead links, moves parts to cheaper vetted UK shops and suggests compatible swaps.

      Prices include VAT. The headline price is the cheapest in-stock offer from a vetted UK shop, ranked on the item plus delivery to Northern Ireland for a single-item order; buying several parts from one shop together usually saves on delivery. Marketplace sellers, used stock and unvetted shops are left out.

      Buttons “Bought it”, “Approve” and “Not for me” open a pre-filled email to yourself; the next morning's sweep reads it and updates the build.
